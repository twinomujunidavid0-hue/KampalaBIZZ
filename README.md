KampalaBIZZ

KampalaBIZZ is a digital business directory platform designed to help people in Kampala easily discover local businesses and services online. It connects customers with nearby businesses while giving small business owners an affordable way to increase their online visibility.

The Problem

Many small businesses in Kampala rely mainly on word-of-mouth and physical signage, making it difficult for customers to find them online. At the same time, many business owners cannot afford expensive advertising or professional websites, limiting their ability to reach new customers.

Core Pillars of KampalaBIZZ
1. Business Discovery

Helping customers easily browse and find local businesses by category, location, services, and contact information.

2. Online Visibility

Giving small businesses a free and accessible online presence so they can be discovered by more customers.

3. Local Connectivity

Connecting communities with trusted neighborhood businesses and making local services easier to access.

4. Affordable Digital Access

Providing a low-cost alternative to expensive advertising and website development for small business owners.
